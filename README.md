A 3D open-world crime game set in a working fishing port and night market. You run a market-stall smuggling ring for Doña Carmen Salas: duck behind stalls to break police line of sight, manage your reputation row by row, steal dock cars while stallholders phone the Policía Portuaria, and work 7 staged story missions across 5 districts.

## Controls
- WASD move · Shift sprint · Space snap to cover / duck behind a stall
- Mouse aim · Click or F shoot · R reload · 1–6 weapon wheel
- E interact / steal car (prompt: E — STEAL) · Enter exit car
- Z Elías smoke screen · X Rosa quick fix · C Tito eyes out
- M full map · Tab stats & saves · Esc title

## Features (true numbers)
- 5 named districts: Puerto Pesquero, Mercado Nocturno, Barrio Alto, Muelle Seis, Playa Ceniza
- 7 staged story missions, 20 lost cargo collectibles, 5 wanted stars
- Named Policía Portuaria units (MUELLE-4 and more) plus air unit ALCATRAZ at 4+ stars
- Cover combat with witness system, market reputation per stall row, 2 radio stations
- Golden-hour grade plus a lantern-lit night-market mode
